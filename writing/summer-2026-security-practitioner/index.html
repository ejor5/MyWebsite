Building career equity

This summer, I shifted my mindset from becoming an excellent student to becoming a habitual cybersecurity practitioner. I realized that career growth comes from more than completing courses or earning grades. It comes from repeatedly building, testing, investigating, and producing evidence of real ability.

I completed TryHackMe’s Pre-Security path to strengthen my foundations in Linux, networking, operating systems, and web technologies. I then applied those foundations by building a Wazuh and Sysmon security-monitoring lab, where I worked with real Windows endpoint telemetry and practiced connecting individual events to attacker behavior, detection, and incident response.

The most important change was in my habits. Instead of measuring progress by information consumed, I began measuring it by skills demonstrated, systems built, problems investigated, and results explained. That is how I am building career equity: turning consistent effort into practical ability, documented work, and proof that I can adapt.
