02 / Ultralearning

Learn deeply on purpose

I am an ultralearner. I take responsibility for designing the way I learn instead of waiting for a class, project, or credential to do it for me. I choose a demanding target, identify the smallest set of fundamentals that supports it, and build direct practice around the real skill.

By deep learning, I do not mean machine learning. I mean understanding something beyond recognition: retrieving it without notes, applying it in a real system, finding where my mental model breaks, correcting it, and explaining the result clearly enough that someone else can follow it.

The goal is not to collect information. It is to create durable skill, visible evidence, and the ability to work through a problem when the instructions disappear.

03 / Creative practice

Another way I think

I have played piano since sixth grade, and today I play in a performance setting every week. That rhythm has taught me to prepare carefully, listen closely, recover without losing the thread, and stay composed when the room is paying attention.

I am creative in a lot of ways, but piano remains one of the main ways I express that part of myself. Creativity is also a big part of what makes me a strong critical thinker: it helps me see patterns, question the obvious approach, and keep turning over a difficult idea until I find a better way through.

04 / Daily rhythm

Sleep. Learn. Move. Repeat.

Consistency gives the intense work somewhere to live. My schedule is simple enough to repeat and structured enough to protect both my attention and my body.

  1. 07:30
    Wake up and run

    Start the day moving and clear my head before the first work block.

  2. Morning
    Shower, then deep work

    Use the quietest part of the day for the hardest learning or building task.

  3. Day
    Work or school

    Bring the same focus into production work, classes, and customer-facing responsibilities.

  4. Evening
    Work out

    Take care of my body so the learning pace is sustainable instead of temporary.

  5. Night
    Review, sleep, repeat

    Retrieve the important ideas, note the weak points, recover, and come back tomorrow.

I’m an ultralearner. The repetition is the point.
