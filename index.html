How I work

Make the low-risk choice visible.

I want a reviewer to see how I think when the happy path ends.

  1. 01
    Define ownership

    Name the team context, my contribution, and the limit of the claim.

  2. 02
    Design for failure

    Identify unsafe guesses, exceptions, recovery paths, and manual checkpoints.

  3. 03
    Verify behavior

    Use tests, logs, screenshots, or reproducible steps—not adjectives—to support a result.

  4. 04
    Explain the tradeoff

    Document what changed, why it matters, and what remains uncertain.
